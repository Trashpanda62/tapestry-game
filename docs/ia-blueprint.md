# Tapestry Acres information architecture

## Visitor-question structure

| Route | Primary question | Evidence and next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/` | What is this place? | Family + working-farm welcome, real-photo proof, Book an experience / Stay CTAs |
| `/experiences` | What can I book? | Seven schema-backed experiences; price/duration/status; Square or inquiry path |
| `/animals` | Who will I meet? | Animal groups and named individuals; Meet the Herd entry point |
| `/herd` | What should I expect? | Herd personalities, visit norms, practical first-visit guidance |
| `/stay` | How do I stay? | RV/farm-stay options, current facts, location, inquiry/reservation path |
| `/shop` | How do I buy? | Searchable catalog families, filters, product detail, Square checkout |
| `/thanks` | Did my request arrive? | Plain confirmation and one next step back to the farm |

Each route has one visible H1, a short orientation paragraph, proof before persuasion, and one dominant next action. “Animals” is the browse/index entry; “Meet the Herd” (`/herd`) is the story and visit-context entry—never two competing labels for the same job. Practical details (location, timing, accessibility, what to bring, and contact) sit beside the relevant decision instead of being hidden in a generic footer.
