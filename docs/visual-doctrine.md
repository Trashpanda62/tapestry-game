# Tapestry Acres visual doctrine

## Tokens

- `pasture`: `#2F5D50` — primary action, navigation, and deep farm green.
- `clay`: `#B86B4B` — warm accent for calls to action, tags, and illustrated marks.
- `sky`: `#BFD9D3` — open background field and quiet information panels.
- `ochre`: `#D39B3F` — highlight, availability, and small moments of energy.
- `ink`: `#1F2A27` — readable text and linework; never pure black.
- `paper`: `#F5F0E6` — tactile field-note surface; use sparingly behind content.

## Type and surfaces

- Pair a sturdy, friendly display face with a workhorse body face. Display headings should feel hand-set and confident; body copy stays plain, compact, and highly readable. Do not depend on generic Georgia or a decorative script.
- Use field-label language for metadata: compact uppercase labels, stamped dividers, handwritten-adjacent notes, and practical captions. Labels clarify provenance, season, and next action; they do not replace descriptive copy.
- Keep real farm photography primary for trust and proof. Use the illustrated field kit as a small layer of texture, wayfinding, and warmth around photos—not as a substitute for animal, host, guest, fiber, or RV evidence.

## Motion and restraint

- Default motion is still. Allow only short, purposeful reveals or gentle hover feedback that helps orientation; no autoplay spectacle, parallax dependency, or looping decorative motion.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` by disabling transitions and transforms.
- Avoid diffuse shadows, glassy gradients, beige-on-beige layouts, and generic rustic motifs. Prefer crisp borders, modest radii, visible focus states, and generous but practical spacing.
