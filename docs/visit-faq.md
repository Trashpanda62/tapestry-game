# Before your visit (verified)

## Questions we can answer

- **What should I wear?** Closed-toe shoes anywhere on the farm, not only for Coffee with the Cows. Every experience puts visitors outdoors on pasture and farm paths. [Owner correction, Steve, 2026-07-29 — supersedes the earlier Coffee-with-the-Cows-only wording taken from C2/C7]
- **When is the farm open?** Visits are bookable March 1 to June 30 and September 1 to December 31, 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Central. The booking service reports the same window in the `season` object it returns with availability, and off-season dates route to a private-visit email. [C2, booking service]
- **How do I choose a date?** Five appointment-based experiences have live availability in their booking calendars. Days with open times are highlighted; choose one, pick a time, hold it for ten minutes, then pay on-site through Square. [C2]
- **How long should I plan?** Farm Shopping is 30 minutes; Alpaca Experience and Virtual Farm Tour are one hour; Birthday Party is 90 minutes; Private Guided Farm Tour is two hours. Coffee with the Cows runs on Saturday mornings. [C2]
- **Where is the farm?** Tapestry Acres is at 396 Taylor Crossroads Rd, Monroe, TN. [C6]
- **How do I ask about a visit?** Email tapestryacres@gmail.com or call (931) 823-3266. [C6]

## Questions that stay human

Ask the farm team about weather, accessibility, cancellation terms, or child-policy details before booking so the answer fits your group and the day. Coffee with the Cows specifically welcomes kids. [C2, C7]
