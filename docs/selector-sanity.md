# Design selector sanity pass

The bounded preview set was reviewed as a matrix at narrow (320–430px), tablet (768px), and desktop (1440px) widths.

- Split, documentary, and illustrated hero variants keep the primary CTA readable over the image and collapse to one column below 700px.
- Pasture/ochre, clay/sky, and ink/alfalfa palettes keep text and action contrast inside the light theme.
- Sturdy slab, humanist field-note, and condensed farm-label type options change headings without changing copy measure or body readability.
- Paper label, painted sign, and clean field guide surfaces preserve visible borders and focus states.
- Comfortable/compact density remains usable on touch; still/subtle/lively motion is overridden by `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Rail and field-tab navigation treatments remain a single, reachable landmark. No combination is permitted to hide the next action or introduce horizontal overflow.
