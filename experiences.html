Before you head to the farm

A little planning leaves more time for the animals.

396 Taylor Crossroads Rd, Monroe, TN ↗ Near Dale Hollow Lake (931) 823-3266 tapestryacres@gmail.com
How do I choose a date?

Choose a date on any experience card to see that experience's available times and complete your reservation.

How much time should I plan?

Farm-store visits are 30 minutes. Alpaca and virtual experiences are one hour, birthday parties are 90 minutes, and private farm tours are two hours. Coffee with the Cows runs Saturday mornings.

What should I wear?

Closed-toe shoes are recommended for Coffee with the Cows. For another experience—or if the forecast looks questionable—ask the farm before you leave home.

What should I confirm before booking?

Contact the farm about weather, accessibility, cancellation terms, or child policies so the answer fits your group and the day of your visit.
