## Anti-patterns verdict

**Pass.** Detector reports three warnings, all per-file scans blind to the shared stylesheet: `single-font` reads one page-local `<style>` block where `--serif` is the only family named, and two `flat-type-hierarchy` hits measure form and utility microcopy rather than the `clamp()` heading scale in `site.css`. Nothing was restyled to satisfy them.

The standing tell is unchanged: **four near-identical card grids** on one page — hub, featured experiences, shop preview, gift certificates. The proof band was deliberately built as an asymmetric split rather than a fifth.

## What's working

1. **The calendar now tells the truth.** It shows which days have openings, how many, and when a party size is what emptied the grid. It replaced a control that defaulted to an out-of-season date and made visitors guess.
2. **The proof band is real all the way down.** A verifiable 4.3 linked to the listing, a follower count that can be checked, and no invented quotes in the gap where quotes are missing.
3. **The mobile shell finally works as one thing.** Menu, skip link, footer nav, social and the call bar all come from `site-shell.js`, so a fix lands on eleven routes at once — which is exactly how the four-page dead-menu bug got fixed in one edit.

## Priority issues

### [P2] The hub grid still spends the best fold on navigation

**Why it matters:** 809px on a phone, directly under the hero, to restate four destinations already in the header. It is the last structural thing standing between a visitor and a reason to come.
**Fix:** Cut it, or replace it with the visit facts that currently sit 9,000px down.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable distill`

### [P2] Twelve screens on a phone

**Why it matters:** The page grew as proof and season copy were added. Nothing on it is filler now, but length itself is a cost, and the shop and experiences previews account for 3,374px of it.
**Fix:** Cap the shop preview at three, or make both previews horizontal scrollers on mobile.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

### [P3] No verbatim reviews

**Why it matters:** The band shows a rating and a link. A stranger deciding whether to bring their kids reads sentences, not averages.
**Fix:** Three real quotes from the Google Business Profile dashboard, with names.

## Persona red flags

**Casey (distracted mobile):** Call and directions are one thumb-tap from anywhere. Still scrolls twelve screens if they want the whole page, and the shop preview is four cards deep on the way.

**Riley (stress tester):** Finds the gift-certificate tiers still resolve to one URL. Finds a 2px horizontal overflow on experiences at 320px. Kills JavaScript and gets a real sentence with a working link in both feed slots, which is the correct answer.

## Questions to consider

- If the page could only keep six sections, which two go?
